About Us

Uday Consultancy Services

Uday Consultancy Services has more than a decade of experience in providing consultancy services for a wide range of accreditations, certifications, recognitions, and regulatory approvals.

We work with multinational corporations, FMCG companies, manufacturing industries, food testing laboratories, environmental testing laboratories, and other testing and calibration facilities. Our technically proficient team possesses extensive experience in NABL-accredited laboratories and management systems, including ISO/IEC 17025, ISO 9001, QMS, GLP, and other related laboratory standards.

Our strength lies in our qualified technical experts and experienced consultants who support organizations across diverse sectors and testing disciplines. We provide practical, end-to-end consultancy services—from application, documentation, and implementation to audit management and successful accreditation approval—ensuring a smooth and efficient accreditation journey.
